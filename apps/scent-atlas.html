Perfume from everywhere

Perfume houses, attar makers and incense masters from Asia first, then the Gulf, Africa, the Americas, Oceania and indie Europe. Every entry explains the signature scent, its notes from first spray to dry-down, and what it costs, with Canadian-dollar estimates.

Theme

Tap a country, then a city, a house, its signature scent and any single note. Tapping a note lights up every house in the world that uses it.

To move the map: drag with a mouse, use two fingers on a phone, or the + and − buttons. One finger scrolls the page. Pins are placed on each house's city; houses marked "country" have no exact city on record. Colours show each house's main style. Prices are as last checked; CAD figures are estimates.
